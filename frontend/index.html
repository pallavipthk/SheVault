UIDAI BEST PRACTICE

                
How to Create a Masked Aadhaar

                

                  A Masked Aadhaar masks the first 8 digits of your Aadhaar number, leaving only the last 4 digits visible. It is legally valid and recognized by government and corporate bodies for KYC.
                

                
    
                  
  1. Visit the official UIDAI portal at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in.
    2. 
                  
  2. Click on Download Aadhaar.
    3. 
                  
  3. Check the box: "Do you want a masked Aadhaar?"
    4. 
                  
  4. Enter OTP sent to your linked mobile number and download the password-protected PDF.
    5. 
                  
  5. Add a handwritten or digital cross watermark: "FOR IDENTITY VERIFICATION AT [COMPANY NAME] ONLY".
    6.
